# Research 001 limitations

1. Kaggle is a distribution channel, not the originating exchange.
2. Provenance depends materially on uploader descriptions and frozen page snapshots.
3. Complete historical risk-free and dividend curves may be unavailable.
4. The vendor IV and Greeks model specification may be incomplete.
5. Similar schemas do not establish that separate Kaggle datasets are homogeneous.
6. The claimed EOD snapshot time must still be verified from fields and timestamps.
7. Split adjustment can differ across files and datasets.
8. The sample does not represent the full US options market.
9. The 2020-2022 cross-underlying window overweights pandemic and high-volatility conditions.
10. The five-underlying cross section is exploratory evidence, not a broad population estimate.
