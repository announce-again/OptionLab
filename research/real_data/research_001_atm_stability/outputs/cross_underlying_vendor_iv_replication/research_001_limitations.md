# Research 001B limitations

- Kaggle is a distribution platform; the uploader pages do not name the original data vendor.
- Vendor IV is used for this cross-underlying replication and is not treated as truth.
- QQQ has no 2020 observations in the downloaded file.
- Bid/ask IV uncertainty is unavailable in vendor-IV replication.
- The five-name universe and pandemic-heavy window make inference exploratory.
- One-way clustering with five underlyings is fragile; regression p-values are not confirmatory.
- Snapshot time is uploader-described as 4:00 pm EST but not independently timestamp-verified here.
