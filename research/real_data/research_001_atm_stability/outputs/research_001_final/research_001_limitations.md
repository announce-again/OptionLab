# Research 001 limitations

1. Kaggle is a distribution channel, not an exchange or a unified vendor.
2. The long SPY page names OptionsDX; the Kyle Graupe pages do not name an original vendor.
3. Historical rate and dividend curves are not yet enriched for the full sample.
4. Vendor IV and Greek model details are incomplete and vendor IV is not treated as truth.
5. Similar schemas do not prove identical processing, adjustment, or snapshot policies.
6. EOD timing is uploader-described and not independently timestamp-verified.
7. Split-crossing changes are excluded; strike-grid split diagnostics remain imperfect because grids and expiries change.
8. The research does not represent the full US options market.
9. The 2020–2022 cross-section is pandemic-heavy.
10. Five underlyings and five regression clusters support exploratory, not confirmatory, inference.
11. QQQ is absent in 2020 despite the dataset title.
12. True constant-maturity construction awaits Stage 3.3.
